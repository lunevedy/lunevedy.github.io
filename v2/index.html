Web page block generators

Design a hero and content sections in your browser, then copy the HTML into your own page in VS Code. These tools work best on a laptop or desktop screen.

  1. 1

    Create your page

    Use the starter page generator and save the file in your project-folder.

  2. 2

    Design your blocks

    Choose a hero and some sections. Edit the text and settings in each generator.

  3. 3

    Copy and paste

    Copy each block from its generator and paste it into your page in VS Code.
